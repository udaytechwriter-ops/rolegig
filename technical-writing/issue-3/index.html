    How this list is put together

    Listings come from LinkedIn Jobs, Naukri, company career pages, remote job boards (Himalayas, Remotive, We Work Remotely, Jobicy, Real Work From Anywhere), the Hacker News hiring thread and a LinkedIn post search, collected on 19 October 2026. LinkedIn and Naukri listings are from the past seven days; career pages and remote boards can carry older roles, and each entry shows the day it was posted. Experience figures, pay and the notes on who can apply are taken from each listing, and applicant counts are LinkedIn's or Naukri's on that day, so check the listing before you apply. Entries described as posts are public LinkedIn posts, and the contact details are in the posts.
