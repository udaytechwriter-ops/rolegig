    How this list is put together

    Listings come from LinkedIn Jobs, Naukri, company career pages, remote job boards (Himalayas, We Work Remotely, Real Work From Anywhere), the Hacker News hiring thread and a LinkedIn post search, limited to the past seven days and collected on 12 October 2026. Experience figures, pay and the notes on who can apply are taken from each listing, and applicant counts are LinkedIn's or Naukri's on that day, so check the listing before you apply. Entries described as posts are public LinkedIn posts, and the contact details are in the posts.
