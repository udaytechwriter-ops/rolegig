This week

  • Hyderabad had far more product roles than writing roles this week. The searches found 75 product manager and product owner roles there and 11 for technical writers.
  • Five of the Hyderabad product roles below showed fewer than 75 applicants on LinkedIn on 5 October: Sonatype (31), PDI Technologies (32), Leadrat (66), TriNet (71) and insightsoftware (74).

    How this list is put together

    Listings come from company career pages, Naukri, LinkedIn Jobs, Upwork, Internshala, remote job boards (Himalayas, We Work Remotely, Real Work From Anywhere), the Hacker News hiring thread, a LinkedIn post search and Google searches limited to the past seven days, collected on 5 October 2026. Experience figures are taken from each job description, and applicant counts are LinkedIn's or Naukri's on that day, so check the listing before you apply. Entries described as posts are public LinkedIn or X posts, and the contact details are in the posts.
