RoleGig/Privacy

Privacy

What RoleGig collects, who handles it, and how to leave. Last updated 19 October 2026.

Your email address

When you sign up, your email address is stored by Kit (kit.com), the service that sends the weekly email. It is used to send you the list and nothing else. It is not sold or shared.

Every email has an unsubscribe link. You can also write to hello@rolegig.com and ask for your address to be removed.

This website

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which keeps standard server logs. Pages load fonts from Google Fonts, and pages with a sign-up form load a script from Kit, so those services receive your browser's IP address when a page loads. Kit's script may set its own cookies. RoleGig itself sets no cookies and runs no analytics or advertising.

Links to job listings

Each entry links to a listing on another site, such as LinkedIn, Naukri or a company's career page. Those sites have their own privacy policies.

Questions: hello@rolegig.com.
