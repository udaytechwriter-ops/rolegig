This week

  • This is the first issue laid out by region. Besides India it lists roles in the United States, Canada, Europe, Australia and the Gulf, each with a line on who can apply.
  • Hyderabad again had the most product roles in India. The searches found 65 product manager and product owner roles there, and the 12 below are the pick.
  • Three roles outside India state relocation help: JetBrains in Madrid, Satispay in Milan and Huspy in Dubai.
  • In the United States list, four of the eight roles say they do not sponsor visas: Palo Alto Networks, USAA, EchoStar and Zelis.
  • The Gulf roles are in the UAE, Saudi Arabia and Qatar. The searches found no product roles in Bahrain, Kuwait or Oman this week, and none of the Gulf listings states pay.
  • Hiring posts now come from outside India too. This week they are from Australia, Canada, Germany and the United States, next to four from India.

    How this list is put together

    Listings come from LinkedIn Jobs, Naukri, company career pages, remote job boards (Himalayas, We Work Remotely, Real Work From Anywhere), the Hacker News hiring thread and a LinkedIn post search, limited to the past seven days and collected on 12 October 2026. Experience figures, pay and the notes on who can apply are taken from each listing, and applicant counts are LinkedIn's or Naukri's on that day, so check the listing before you apply. Entries described as posts are public LinkedIn posts, and the contact details are in the posts.
