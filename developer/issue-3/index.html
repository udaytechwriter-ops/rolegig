This week

  • This is the first Developer issue. It covers backend, frontend, full stack and mobile developers, and leaves out DevOps, SAP, Salesforce and support roles.
  • React appears in almost every full stack listing, usually with TypeScript and Node.js. In Hyderabad, Java with Spring Boot and microservices comes up most.
  • Seven of the eight US roles state pay. Deloitte is the only one to mention sponsorship, and it says limited sponsorship may be available.
  • Satispay in Milan and Optiver in Sydney offer relocation, and Optiver also sponsors visas where needed.
  • Several listings now name AI coding tools such as Claude Code, Copilot and Cursor as part of the job.
  • The worldwide list includes a graduate role at Canonical, the company behind Ubuntu.

    How this list is put together

    Listings come from LinkedIn Jobs, Naukri, company career pages, remote job boards (Himalayas, Remotive, We Work Remotely, Jobicy, Real Work From Anywhere), the Hacker News hiring thread and a LinkedIn post search, collected on 19 October 2026. LinkedIn and Naukri listings are from the past seven days; career pages and remote boards can carry older roles, and each entry shows the day it was posted. Experience figures, pay and the notes on who can apply are taken from each listing, and applicant counts are LinkedIn's or Naukri's on that day, so check the listing before you apply. Entries described as posts are public LinkedIn posts, and the contact details are in the posts.
